**Research Room** is Interactive Intelligence's guided research program. Over the program, you'll go through every stage of a real research project: picking a question, reading the literature, designing and running experiments, analyzing your results, and presenting what you found.

## Why join

- **Do real research, start to finish.** Instead of just reading about research, you'll carry out your own project from first question to final result.
- **Get guidance at every step.** The curriculum breaks the research process into clear stages, each with its own deliverable, so you always know what comes next.
- **Leave with something to show.** You'll finish with a research poster, and possibly a paper.
- **Hear from researchers.** The program includes a guest speaker session.
- **Get feedback from peers.** Peer review is built into the end of the program, before you present your work.

## What you'll produce

Each stage of the program ends with a concrete piece of your project:

1. **Research question**: what you're exploring and why it matters
2. **Literature review write-up**: what's already known and where the gaps are
3. **Experiment design**: how you'll test your question
4. **Statistical analysis**: what your results actually show
5. **Poster**, and possibly a paper: how you share your findings
