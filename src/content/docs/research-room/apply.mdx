## Before you apply

- Browse the research areas ([NLP](/ai/transformers/), [Reinforcement Learning](/ai/deep-reinforcement-learning/), [Neural-CS](/neuroscience/computational-neuroscience/), and [Machine Learning](/ai/neural-networks/)) to get a sense of which topics interest you.
