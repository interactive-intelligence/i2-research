Research Room follows the same arc as a real research project. The program moves through three phases, and each phase builds on the one before it.

<Aside type="note">
The exact schedule and length of the program will be shared with accepted members before the program begins.
</Aside>

## Phase 1: Find your question

<Steps>

1. **Introduction**

   Meet the cohort, learn how the program works, and get an overview of what research actually looks like day to day.

2. **Finding your topic**

   Learn how to explore a field, spot open problems, and identify gaps worth investigating.

   _Deliverable: your research question and why you're exploring it._

3. **Literature review**

   Learn how to find, read, and synthesize papers so you know what's already been done.

   _Deliverable: a literature review write-up._

4. **Guest speaker**

   Hear from a researcher about their work and their path into research.

</Steps>

## Phase 2: Run your experiments

<Steps>

1. **Experiment design**

   Turn your question into a concrete, testable plan.

   _Deliverable: your experiment design._

2. **Running experiments**

   Put your plan into action and collect results.

3. **Analyzing data**

   Figure out what your results actually say, using statistics to back up your conclusions.

   _Deliverable: a statistical analysis of your results._

4. **Repetition**

   Rerun and refine your experiments so your results hold up.

</Steps>

## Phase 3: Share your work

<Steps>

1. **Poster, paper, and peer review**

   Turn your project into a poster (and possibly a paper), and give and get feedback from other members.

   _Deliverable: a research poster, and possibly a paper._

2. **Publishing**

   Learn how to present and publish your findings.

</Steps>
