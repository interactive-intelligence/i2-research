## I2 Research Room
<br/>

<div class="rr-intro">

**Research Room** is I2's guided research program. You'll go through every stage of a real project: finding a question, reviewing the literature, designing and running experiments, analyzing results, and presenting a poster. Projects span NLP, reinforcement learning, Neural-CS, and machine learning.
